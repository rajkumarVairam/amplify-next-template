We're excited to introduce **YourApp** — a modern, cloud-native platform built to help teams move faster without sacrificing security or reliability.

## Why we built this

Teams today juggle too many tools. We set out to build a single platform that handles authentication, data, storage, and real-time health monitoring out of the box — so you can focus on what matters: your product.

## What's included

- **Dashboard** — real-time health monitoring for your backend services
- **Profile management** — display name, avatar, theme preference, and notification settings
- **Secure by default** — owner-based authorization on every data record

## What's next

We're just getting started. Over the coming months we'll be shipping:

- Team workspaces
- Custom integrations
- Advanced analytics

Stay tuned, and [reach out](/contact) if you have questions.
