## Accessing your profile

Click **Profile** in the navigation to open your profile settings page.

## Display name and bio

- **Display name** — shown in the navigation instead of your email address. Max 64 characters.
- **Bio** — a short description of yourself. Max 280 characters.

## Avatar

Click **Select Avatar** to choose a JPEG, PNG, or WebP image. Maximum file size is 5 MB. After selecting, click **Upload** to save it to your account.

## Theme preference

Choose between **Light** and **Dark** themes. The change applies immediately across all pages without a page reload.

## Notifications

Toggle **Enable Notifications** to receive in-app alerts when a backend service transitions from Healthy to Unavailable on the dashboard.

## Saving

Click **Save Profile** to persist all changes. Your settings are stored securely in DynamoDB and are tied to your account — they'll be the same on any device you sign in from.
